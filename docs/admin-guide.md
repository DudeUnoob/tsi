# Sanga Content Editor Guide

Welcome! This guide is written for Sanga coordinators and volunteers. It explains how to edit pages, create retreats, post products, and download form submissions without touching any code.

---

## 1. Accessing the Staff Portal

1. Go to the website and add `/admin` to the end of the address in your browser:
   `https://www.sangainitiative.org/admin`
2. Enter your Sanga staff email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

---

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see a dark sidebar on the left containing the editing tabs:

- **Overview**: Displays simple counts of your active retreats, products, and new messages.
- **Homepage Editor**: Controls the main banners, headlines, and call-to-actions.
- **Gatherings**: Create, edit, publish, or delete retreats, camps, trips, and online sessions.
- **Merch Store**: List products or cabin reservations for purchase.
- **Support & Copy**: Manage donation buttons and description text.
- **Community Links**: Update WhatsApp invite URLs and social links.
- **Resources**: Post reading links and downloadable files.
- **Media Library**: Browse images and copy image links to use in event banners.
- **Submissions**: Review contact messages and download subscriber email lists.

---

## 3. Creating & Editing a Gathering

To add a new retreat or online talk:

1. Click on the **Gatherings** tab.
2. Click the **Add Gathering** button at the top right.
3. Fill in the event details:
   - **Title**: The name of the event (e.g., *Camp Ignite 2026*).
   - **Slug**: The web address identifier, in lowercase with dashes (e.g., `camp-ignite-2026`).
   - **Category**: Select if it is a Camp, Retreat, Trip, or Online Talk.
   - **Age Limit**: The target audience age range (e.g., `18–35`).
   - **Price**: Display cost (e.g., `$250` or `Free`).
   - **Dates & Location**: Set the start/end dates and physical address.
   - **Status**:
     - `Draft`: Hidden from the public.
     - `Open`: Shows an active **Register** button.
     - `Coming Soon`: Shows a disabled preview.
     - `Sold Out`: Shows a "Sold Out" indicator.
     - `Past`: Moves the event to the history section.
   - **External Registration URL**: Paste the link to your external registration form (e.g., Squarespace payment page or Stripe Checkout link).
   - **Hero Image URL**: Paste a link to a cover image (you can upload images in the *Media Library* tab to get a link).
4. Check **Publish Immediately** to make it visible on the page, and check **Show Featured on Home** if it should appear on the main landing grid.
5. Click **Save Gathering** at the bottom.

---

## 4. Managing Store Products

To post cabin upgrades or apparel:

1. Navigate to the **Merch Store** tab.
2. Click **Add Product**.
3. Input the product name, price, description, and copy the external checkout URL from Squarespace.
4. Set status to `Available` or `Sold Out`.
5. Click **Save Product**.

---

## 5. Exporting Newsletter & Contact Form Submissions

To review and download email lists:

1. Go to the **Submissions** tab.
2. You will see tables containing your subscribers and contact messages.
3. Click the **Export Subscribers to CSV** or **Export Forms to CSV** buttons.
4. A file will immediately download to your computer which you can open directly in **Excel**, **Google Sheets**, or any spreadsheet program.

---

## 6. Voluntary Tips

- **Image Dimensions**: For best results, use horizontal layout images (e.g., 16:9 ratio) for event covers.
- **WhatsApp Links**: If the central Sanga WhatsApp link changes, update it under the **Community Links** tab to update all buttons across the entire website at once.
- **Formatting Text**: To start a new paragraph in description fields, press Enter twice to leave a clean blank line.
